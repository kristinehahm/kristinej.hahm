About

Hi! I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at the Better Government Lab at Georgetown University McCourt School of Public Policy.

My research examines citizen–state interactions in policy implementation, focusing on barriers that limit access to safety-net programs. I study these barriers through three lenses:

  1. Administrative burden: why eligible individuals fail to take up benefits.
  2. Spatial access: whether services are geographically available and usable for those who need them.
  3. State capacity: whether governments have the resources and infrastructure to reach a policy’s intended beneficiaries.
