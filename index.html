About

Hi! I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at the Better Government Lab at Georgetown University McCourt School of Public Policy. I am also a Ph.D. candidate in Public Policy and Political Economy at the University of Texas at Dallas, where I also earned an M.S. in Geospatial Information Sciences. I hold a Master of Public Policy from Seoul National University. My research examines how administrative burden and geographic access jointly shape participation in safety-net programs and the receipt of health care among low-income populations.

My dissertation, The Pathways to Cancer Care: Knowledge, Administrative Burden, and Medicaid Expansion, links SEER cancer registry records with Medicaid administrative data to estimate how enrollment barriers and proximity to oncology care affect coverage continuity and survival, and how the Medicaid expansion under the ACA reshaped these relationships.
